下面是一套可以直接照着做的 `GPT Image 2` 海报生成流程，适配你的 `80 × 200 cm` 竖版展架。

**生成设置**

如果可以选比例：选 `2:5 竖版`。  
如果可以填尺寸：优先用 `1536 × 3840 px`。  
生成内容：只生成主视觉，不要让 AI 生成中文文字。  
后期文字：用 Canva / PS / PPT / Figma 自己加。

---

**第 1 步：生成无文字主视觉**

直接复制这个提示词：

```text
Create a vertical ultra-tall game poster key visual in a 2:5 aspect ratio, suitable for an 80cm x 200cm exhibition banner.

The poster is for a game called "Physics Brawl", but do not include any text, letters, logos, captions, watermarks, or UI words in the image.

Scene: a futuristic physics laboratory with a floating combat arena viewed from a dramatic slightly top-down angle. The arena is a clean white-gray experimental platform with glowing coordinate grid lines, red warning lights around the edge, and a sense that players can be knocked off the stage.

Main action: three stylized game characters are fighting in the center.
Character 1: a blue heavy armored mech representing classical mechanics, charging forward with a huge blue force arrow, impact shockwaves, and visible motion lines.
Character 2: a yellow electromagnetic energy sphere representing electricity and magnetism, surrounded by red positive charges, blue negative charges, electric arcs, and glowing field lines.
Character 3: a green geometric afterimage character representing mathematics, leaving segmented motion trails, coordinate axes, projection lines, and speed-halving visual effects.

Foreground: several glowing strategy cards flying through the air, plus a large six-sided dice showing the number 6, glowing like a critical experiment result.

Composition: leave clean empty space at the top for a large title, and clean empty space at the bottom for feature text and a QR code. Keep the central battle action dynamic and readable. Strong depth, cinematic lighting, vivid but clean sci-fi colors, high-quality game promotional art, sharp silhouettes, clear visual hierarchy, polished esports poster style.

Color palette: deep navy and dark laboratory background, white-gray arena, blue mechanics effects, yellow electric effects, green math effects, red danger edge lighting, gold-white dice highlight.

Avoid: no text, no letters, no logo, no watermark, no cluttered tiny symbols, no realistic humans, no horror, no blood, no messy composition.
```

---

**第 2 步：如果画面太乱，用这个修正**

```text
Revise the image to make the composition cleaner and more readable. Keep the same 2:5 vertical poster layout, same futuristic physics arena, same three science-themed characters, same cards and dice. Reduce visual clutter, make the characters more distinct, keep the top and bottom empty spaces clear for later text placement. Do not add any text, letters, logos, or watermark.
```

**第 3 步：如果不够像游戏海报，用这个修正**

```text
Make the image feel more like a polished commercial game key art poster. Increase the dramatic action, improve lighting contrast, make the arena edge danger more obvious, make the flying cards and glowing dice more prominent, and keep the three characters visually distinct. Preserve the 2:5 vertical layout and the empty spaces at top and bottom. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark.
```

**第 4 步：如果顶部/底部没留空间，用这个修正**

```text
Adjust the layout for an exhibition banner. Keep the main battle scene in the middle 60% of the poster. Leave a clean, darker empty area at the top for a large title and a clean empty area at the bottom for selling points and a QR code. Do not add any text or logo.
```

**后期加字建议**

顶部大标题：

```text
力学大乱斗
```

副标题：

```text
用公式出牌，把对手弹出实验台
```

底部卖点：

```text
物理碰撞乱斗
卡牌策略构筑
掷骰随机结算
多人实验竞技
```

最后印刷前，把画布设为 `80.6 × 200.6 cm`，四边留 `3 mm` 出血，重要文字离边缘至少 `3-5 cm`。生成图不够大时，先用超分辨率放大，再放进最终海报画布里排版。
